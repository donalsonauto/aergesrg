# Cost per lead and cost per sale
Type: best-practice. Tags: budget, cost per lead, cost per sale, ROI.

Cost per lead is spend divided by leads; cost per sale is spend divided by sales. Only compute them where spend is known for every month in the window; a partial sum understates spend and overstates ROI. Compare sources on cost per sale, not cost per lead: a cheap lead that never closes is the expensive one.

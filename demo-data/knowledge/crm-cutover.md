# What a CRM cutover looks like in the data
Type: troubleshooting. Tags: integration, leads, cutover, feed loss.

When a rooftop switches CRM, a whole lead feed often goes to zero for one lead type (usually internet) while every other store keeps reporting. The signature: at one store, every source of one lead type drops to zero leads in the same month and stays there. The store's total sales do not fall in step; they show up under another source, usually walk-in, because the customers still came in and the new CRM logged them differently.

That is feed loss, not lost business. Treat it as a data-quality finding: say which store, which lead type, and the first missing month, and ask for the integration status or an independent sales export before concluding anything about demand. Do not count those sources as recoverable revenue and do not recommend cancelling a vendor because its feed broke.

In the demo group, Summit Hyundai Oakhurst has exactly this pattern: its internet sources stop mid-window and its Walk In sales jump the same month.

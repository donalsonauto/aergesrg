# Reading a closing ratio drop
Type: troubleshooting. Tags: closing ratio, leads, sales.

A closing ratio usually falls for one of three reasons: more leads of lower quality (ratio down, leads up, sales flat), fewer sales on the same leads (ratio down, leads flat, sales down), or a feed change that started counting a new kind of lead (ratio down sharply the month a source's lead count jumps). Check the three in that order. Decompose by source first, then by store.

A drop isolated to one store points at something local: a process change, a staffing change, a source-mix shift, a reporting change. It does not prove which. Report the measured change, say which of the three patterns the numbers match, and name what would confirm it (the store's lead-status breakdown, the source mix that month, whether the CRM changed).

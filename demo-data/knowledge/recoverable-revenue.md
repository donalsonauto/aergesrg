# When the recoverable revenue estimate is unreliable
Type: best-practice. Tags: recoverable revenue, dormant, estimate.

The estimate assumes a source returns to its own historical run rate at its own average gross. It is a planning figure, not a forecast. Do not trust it for a source with fewer than five total sales, for a source whose history is under twelve months, or for a source that was a one-off campaign (a single burst, then nothing). Say the window on screen. A dealer will take the number into a vendor negotiation.
